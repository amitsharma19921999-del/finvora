Install Finvora on iPhone / iPad

Add Finvora to your Home Screen in a few taps.

  1. Open this site in Safari (not Chrome) — the ⎙ Share button must be visible.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an up-arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" — Finvora installs like a normal app with its own icon.
iOS doesn't support APK files, so iPhone users install the web app (PWA) this way — it works fully, including live prices.

Not seeing "Add to Home Screen"? Make sure you're in Safari and reload the page.
